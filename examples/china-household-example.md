# Example: China household mode

This is illustrative only. Rates and company practices must be re-verified for the actual year, city, payroll base, and offer.

## Scenario
Candidate compares:
- Offer A: Hangzhou algorithm role, CNY 28k × 14 target; high housing-fund contribution; can live with partner.
- Offer B: Shanghai role, CNY 32k × 15 headline; lower housing-fund contribution; 3 months are performance-linked; separate housing required.

Current separate housing costs are CNY 3,000/month each. Living together near a midpoint would cost CNY 4,000/month total.

## Household housing effect
Separate housing: `3,000 + 3,000 = 6,000/month`
Shared housing: `4,000/month`
After-tax household saving: `2,000 × 12 = CNY 24,000/year`

If the relevant marginal take-home rate is about 75%, the gross-salary equivalent is:
`24,000 / 0.75 = CNY 32,000/year`

So an apparently CNY 30k higher gross salary elsewhere may not create a higher household surplus after duplicated rent, travel, tax, and contribution differences.

## Correct presentation
Show four layers separately:
1. Guaranteed and target gross compensation.
2. Risk-adjusted after-tax cash.
3. Wealth accrual including owned housing-fund/pension/equity value.
4. Household surplus after shared or duplicated costs.

Then add WLB, commute, role quality, and career option value. Do not claim one offer is superior unless the user's priorities support that conclusion.
