# Evidence and research

## Evidence matched to the question
There is no universal source ladder. Select evidence for the claim being tested:

| Question | Most useful evidence | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Guaranteed amount, conditions, deadline | Actual written offer/contract and written clarification | Intent or a target bonus is not a guarantee; formal offer labels alone do not settle enforceability |
| Tax, residency, social insurance, housing fund | Current official source for jurisdiction and year | A foreign interview guide is not local tax authority |
| Actual bonus, working hours, leave use | Multiple recent comparable same-team reports, checked against terms | Reports describe a sample, not a future guarantee |
| Duties, mentoring, algorithm resources | Future manager/project discussion, concrete deliverables, team corroboration | JD title, brand and official marketing do not establish equipment/data/compute access or daily work |
| Office costs and travel | Current office-specific rental/transit evidence and site schedule | City averages miss duplicated housing and customer-site work |
| Historical context | Dated campus reports and essays | Author preference and old salary figures are neither universal rules nor current prices |

When evidence cannot be obtained, retain an explicit unknown and a reversal condition. Do not upgrade a guess to a fact because a numeric score is requested.

## Freshness
- Tax, payroll, statutory contribution, and housing rules: verify for the relevant calendar year and jurisdiction.
- Rent and commute: prefer current listings or current transit estimates near the actual office, not city-wide averages.
- Offer bands: prefer same role/level/location and the latest 1–2 recruiting cycles.
- Work hours: prefer the same business unit/team. Company-wide reputation is a fallback, not proof.

## Evidence labels
Use inline labels or a source column:
- `Offer`: user's actual written terms.
- `Official`: government/company official source.
- `Employee report`: first-person or credible employee/candidate report.
- `Historical`: past-cycle offer data.
- `Assumption`: explicit modeling choice.

## Handling conflicts
Do not average incompatible claims automatically. Explain likely reasons: team, year, location, level, target vs guaranteed bonus, base vs supplemental benefits, or different payroll bases. Model conservative/base/upside cases when needed.

For example, a written target of four bonus months, an oral assurance of four, and a recent same-team payout of one support separate guarantee/target/observed scenarios. They do not establish an expected payout probability. Ask for the one clarification that changes the recommendation, such as a written minimum, within the 1–3-question turn budget. Record date, scope and unresolved conflict alongside the affected table cell. Prefer a conditional recommendation over waiting for exhaustive research.

## Common traps
- "15 salaries" where 3 months are performance bonus.
- Total equity grant counted as annual cash.
- Employer contribution counted as spendable take-home.
- Social benefits treated as tax instead of personal assets/insurance.
- Gross rent savings compared 1:1 with gross salary.
- City average rent used instead of the office/commute corridor.
- Company-wide WLB reputation applied to a specific team without qualification.
- Treating intent, a conversion internship or unmet offer conditions as assured employment.
- Letting pay/brand scores compensate for a user's confirmed hard constraint violation.
- Assuming all algorithm titles involve model research or all small firms lack mentors.
- Counting included subsidies, reimbursed costs, sold equity or embedded on-call hours twice.
- Mixing joining calendar-year pay with full-year costs/hours or treating clawback cash as permanent income.
- Repeating supplied or declined personal questions; broad question banks disguised as three numbered questions.

## Reading sources and reuse boundaries
These links motivate questions, not company ratings or live compensation estimates. Reviewed 2026-10-04. Summaries below are original; no source text, screenshots, offer letters or personal profiles are redistributed.

- [阿秀：如何从多维度评估 offer](https://interviewguide.cn/notes/05-xiustar/06-offer/01-offer_choose.html): a personal perspective on role, team/platform, location and compensation. Use it to prompt role/team verification. Its views on company size, cities and choices are author opinions, not universal facts; historical example salaries are not current benchmarks.
- [牛客：24 届 CV / 自驾算法岗秋招总结与经验分享](https://www.nowcoder.com/discuss/587426823147995136), dated 2024-02-14: a pseudonymous campus applicant's retrospective, useful for technical fit, mentorship and employment-risk questions. Do not generalize one applicant's results or recreate their identity/background in tests. It is historical self-report, not validated market pricing.
- [viraptor/reverse-interview](https://github.com/viraptor/reverse-interview): supplementary general prompts about daily work, onboarding, mentorship, delivery and on-call. Select and rewrite only relevant questions. Do not import foreign benefits/tax assumptions or dump the whole list on the user.

Keep the repository's MIT notice for its original material. External pages retain their own rights; citing a page does not relicense it. New examples and test cases must be explicitly fictional and must not include private user conversation details.
