# Evidence and research

## Source hierarchy
Use evidence in roughly this order:
1. Signed offer / written recruiter terms / employment contract.
2. Current official tax, social-insurance, pension, housing-fund, benefits, and company policy sources.
3. Multiple recent first-person employee or candidate reports for actual payout, hours, team culture, and offer bands.
4. Historical offer databases and campus-offer compilations.
5. Recruiting JD ranges and recruiter marketing.
6. Anonymous single-source claims or model assumptions.

A lower-ranked source can still be useful for company practice that official sources do not disclose. Label it.

## Freshness
- Tax, payroll, statutory contribution, and housing rules: verify for the relevant calendar year and jurisdiction.
- Rent and commute: prefer current listings or current transit estimates near the actual office, not city-wide averages.
- Offer bands: prefer same role/level/location and the latest 1–2 recruiting cycles.
- Work hours: prefer the same business unit/team. Company-wide reputation is a fallback, not proof.

## Evidence labels
Use inline labels or a source column:
- `Offer`: user's actual written terms.
- `Official`: government/company official source.
- `Employee report`: first-person or credible employee/candidate report.
- `Historical`: past-cycle offer data.
- `Assumption`: explicit modeling choice.

## Handling conflicts
Do not average incompatible claims automatically. Explain likely reasons: team, year, location, level, target vs guaranteed bonus, base vs supplemental benefits, or different payroll bases. Model conservative/base/upside cases when needed.

## Common traps
- "15 salaries" where 3 months are performance bonus.
- Total equity grant counted as annual cash.
- Employer contribution counted as spendable take-home.
- Social benefits treated as tax instead of personal assets/insurance.
- Gross rent savings compared 1:1 with gross salary.
- City average rent used instead of the office/commute corridor.
- Company-wide WLB reputation applied to a specific team without qualification.
