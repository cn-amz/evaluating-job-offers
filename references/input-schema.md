# Input schema

Collect only fields that can change the decision. Unknown fields remain unknown; do not invent them.

## Intake order and privacy
Reuse conversation facts first. Record priorities and hard constraints before financial scoring; show pass/fail/pending for each option. Mainland China campus technical roles are the primary intake route, but route other locations through their own employment, residency and tax context.

Ask only 1–3 unresolved high-impact questions per turn (zero when sufficient, fewer if requested). Rank by whether the answer could reverse the decision before a deadline. One question addresses one uncertainty; do not bundle all fields below into a numbered checklist. Record remaining unknowns without demanding answers. Do not repeat known/declined questions or infer family plans. Budget totals/ranges and a household-mode opt-out are sufficient; names, exact home addresses, identification numbers and relationship history are unnecessary.

## Offer fields
- company, role, level, employment type
- status: intent / conversion internship / conditional offer / formal employment offer; written terms, unmet conditions, approvals, acceptance deadline and start-date certainty
- conversion path: funded headcount, assessment criteria, decision date, internship pay/duration, non-conversion fallback; separate from formal employment probation
- hard constraints: acceptable locations, maximum commute/site travel, dependable income floor, work authorization and other user-stated non-negotiables
- office location(s), remote/hybrid policy, expected start date
- monthly/annual base salary and currency
- fixed salary months or guaranteed annual base
- target/max bonus; whether contractual; historical payout if known
- sign-on / relocation / retention bonus and clawback terms
- equity type, grant size, vesting schedule, liquidity, current valuation/price, refresh policy
- overtime pay, commission, allowances, meal/transport/housing subsidies
- probation salary differences
- pension/retirement match, provident/housing fund, employer social contributions, insurance premiums
- PTO, sick leave, public holidays, on-call/travel expectations
- expected weekly hours and commute
- actual tasks and first deliverable; research/training vs integration, testing, deployment, after-sales or customer-site work; site frequency/duration and who controls assignments
- named role of mentor (personal identity unnecessary), review time/cadence and availability; accessible equipment, authorized data, compute budget and deployment ownership
- relevant project continuity, team turnover and runway evidence; do not assume large company means good mentoring or small company means poor growth

## Personal/household fields
- tax residency and work jurisdiction
- housing preference: shared room / one-bedroom / family housing / owned home
- likely rent near office and acceptable commute
- partner/family location and whether cohabitation changes housing costs
- duplicated housing or cross-city travel costs
- childcare/caregiving or dependent costs when relevant
- user's priorities: cash, savings, time, city, relationship/family, role fit, learning, stability, prestige, upside
- time horizon: year 1, steady state, 3–4 years
- precise period: joining calendar year (dates/months), first complete 12 months, steady year; currency and FX date/scenarios if needed
- whether cash already includes allowances or sold-equity proceeds; whether budget totals already include rent, travel or reimbursements; whether hours already include on-call/travel and weeks exclude leave

## Confidence fields
For every important input record:
- value
- source: Offer / Official / Employee report / Historical / Assumption
- date
- confidence: high / medium / low
- scope: team, role/level, city, period and what specific claim the source supports
- included-in: cash total / asset balance / expense total / hours / not yet included (to prevent double counting)

If the user supplies a signed offer or recruiter-written terms, treat those specific terms as higher confidence than public anecdotes, while still separating contractual guarantees from target/variable amounts.
For actual practice, compare recent same-team reports and project evidence; written target compensation does not establish realized payout. A written conditional offer remains conditional until its conditions are checked.
